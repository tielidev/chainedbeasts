## What are all the beasts in Chained Beasts?

Chained Beasts has **eight playable beasts**: the Rhino, Ibex, Wolf, Panther, Bear, Eagle, Lion and Monkey. Featherweight Games announces "8 different beasts" with variants, and the game's Steam achievement list names all eight in its "Free as a …" achievements (win a run as that beast).

Each beast comes in **three variants**. A variant is not just a skin: its description changes how the beast plays, such as extra health, a damage bonus or a crowd-pleasing effect. Cosmetics are a separate unlock track.

| Beast | How to unlock | First variant shown | What that variant does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rhino | Available from the start | Armored Rhino | Shields last twice as long; takes 30% less damage |
| Ibex | Available from the start | Agile Ibex | Faster movement; dodges cost 50% less stamina |
| Wolf | Survive Carthage | Ferocious Wolf | More dismemberments; +200% damage with enemy body parts |
| Panther | Survive Germania | Deadly Panther | More damage, less health; +50% special attack damage |
| Bear | Survive Rome | Hulking Bear | More health, less movement speed; brief invulnerability and fury when hit |
| Eagle | Survive the Senate | Lethal Eagle | Powerful throw after melee kills |
| Lion | Win a run on Legionnaire difficulty | Regal Lion / Proud Lion | Posing to a wild crowd after kills makes them throw coins / boosts allies |
| Monkey | Win a run on Primus difficulty | Clever, Curious or Vicious Monkey | Reroll Trainer skills, plus a variant-specific twist |

The unlock conditions and variant descriptions are read from the beast-select screen in launch gameplay videos (sources below). We list only variant names that appear on screen; where a third variant has not been confirmed, we say so rather than guess.

## How do you unlock new beasts?

Two beasts are open from your first run. The other six are tied to campaign progress or to winning on higher difficulty levels:

1. **Wolf**: survive Carthage, the first act.
2. **Panther**: survive Germania, the second act.
3. **Bear**: survive Rome, the third act.
4. **Eagle**: survive the Senate, the final stage after Rome.
5. **Lion**: complete a run on Legionnaire difficulty.
6. **Monkey**: complete a run on Primus difficulty.

That order means most players meet the Wolf and Panther during their first serious attempts, while the Eagle, Lion and Monkey are rewards for finishing runs. The Steam achievement **Bestiary** (unlock all beasts) was held by about 0.1% of players a few days after launch. For the campaign route itself, see [acts and campaign progression](/guides/acts-and-progression/), and for difficulty names, see [difficulty levels](/guides/difficulty-levels/).

## How do beast variants unlock?

On the select screen each beast has three variant slots, labeled I, II and III. Variant I is available as soon as the beast is. The locked slots ask you to reach that beast's **level II** and **level III**. The developer's May 2026 update describes the same loop: fight with a beast to earn XP and unlock new variants.

Practical takeaway: if you want a specific variant, keep playing the same beast rather than rotating every run. XP is earned per beast, so spreading runs across all eight slows every individual unlock.

## What does each beast do?

**Rhino.** The Armored Rhino is the most forgiving starter: shields last twice as long and it takes 30% less damage. It suits players who are still learning to block and read enemy attacks.

**Ibex.** The Agile Ibex trades defense for mobility, with faster movement and dodges that cost half the stamina. It rewards players who already dodge well and want to stay out of trouble rather than absorb it.

**Wolf.** Both confirmed Wolf variants revolve around dismemberment. The Ferocious Wolf deals +200% damage when you fight with enemy body parts, while the Bone Collector Wolf earns coins when picking up body parts. Creators running the Wolf lean into limb-based builds.

**Panther.** The Deadly Panther is the glass cannon: more damage, less health and +50% special attack damage. It is strong when you land specials, but mistakes cost more.

**Bear.** The Hulking Bear has more health and less movement speed, plus brief invulnerability and fury when it takes damage. In launch gameplay, MarvGoneRogue built an aggressive, punch-heavy run around that trigger.

**Eagle.** The Lethal Eagle gains a powerful throw after melee kills, which ties it to the game's throwing and weapon-juggling systems.

**Lion.** The Lion's variants work through the crowd. The Regal Lion poses to a wild crowd after kills so the crowd throws coins; the Proud Lion's pose instead gives allies a damage and speed boost, which makes it a natural co-op support pick.

**Monkey.** Every confirmed Monkey variant can reroll Trainer skills. The Clever Monkey starts with two skills and an affliction, the Curious Monkey gets only one skill choice but earns a bonus skill every third level, and the Vicious Monkey adds +50% unarmed damage with an unarmed special that sends enemies flying.

For a ranking of these choices, see our [Chained Beasts tier list](/beasts/tier-list/). For how skills and the Trainer interact with these traits, see [skills, training and shopping](/combat/skills-and-shopping/).

Steam publishes the share of all players who hold each "Free as a …" achievement. These numbers are a snapshot a few days after launch (October 19, 2026), and they mostly reflect **how early each beast unlocks**, not how strong it is: the Rhino and Ibex are available from the start, while the Monkey needs a Primus-difficulty win first.
