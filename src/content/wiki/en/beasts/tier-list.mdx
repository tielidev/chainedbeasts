## What is the best beast in Chained Beasts?

For most players the safest picks are the **Hulking Bear** and the **Armored Rhino**: both turn mistakes into survivable situations, which matters in a game where healing between rounds can cost you an affliction. The Ferocious Wolf, Agile Ibex and Deadly Panther hit harder or move better but punish errors more. The Lion and Monkey are strongest when you build around their specific twist.

This is an **editorial tier list**. We ranked each beast's first variant using the trait text shown on the select screen and what launch gameplay shows those traits doing. It is not datamined, and Featherweight Games has not published balance numbers. See [all beasts and unlocks](/beasts/all-beasts/) for every trait and unlock condition.

| Tier | Beast (variant) | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S | Hulking Bear | More health; brief invulnerability and fury when hit turn damage into a counter-attack window |
| S | Armored Rhino | 30% less damage taken and shields that last twice as long; available from the start |
| A | Ferocious Wolf | +200% damage with enemy body parts gives huge damage once limbs start flying |
| A | Agile Ibex | Faster movement and half-cost dodges; excellent if you dodge well, weaker if you get caught |
| A | Deadly Panther | +50% special attack damage, but less health makes every mistake expensive |
| B | Proud Lion | Team damage and speed boost after posing; much better in co-op than solo |
| B | Lethal Eagle | Powerful throw after melee kills; rewards players who juggle weapons |
| B | Vicious Monkey | +50% unarmed damage and Trainer rerolls; needs an unarmed build to pay off |
| C | Regal Lion | Coins from the crowd help your economy, but add no direct power in a fight |

## Why are the Bear and Rhino at the top?

Chained Beasts makes recovery costly: between rounds, healing can add [afflictions](/combat/afflictions-and-healing/) that change how you move or see. A beast that loses less health in the first place reduces how often you face that trade.

The **Armored Rhino** does this passively: every hit is reduced by 30%, and longer-lasting shields make [blocking](/combat/shields-and-blocking/) more reliable. The **Hulking Bear** does it reactively: getting hit grants brief invulnerability and fury, so a mistake becomes a window to hit back. Its trade-off is lower movement speed, which matters more when you are chained to faster teammates.

## How should you pick a beast for solo play?

Alone, nobody revives you, distracts enemies or covers a bad position. Favor beasts whose traits work without a team:

- **Best solo picks:** Hulking Bear, Armored Rhino.
- **High skill ceiling:** Agile Ibex (if you dodge consistently), Deadly Panther (if you land special attacks).
- **Weaker solo:** Proud Lion, whose boost is aimed at allies.

Our [solo guide](/guides/solo-guide/) covers solo positioning and recovery in more detail.

## How should you pick beasts for co-op?

In [co-op](/co-op/multiplayer-guide/) you are physically chained together, so the team's slowest and most fragile member shapes every fight. A balanced party pairs one durable front-liner with damage dealers:

- **Front line:** Hulking Bear or Armored Rhino.
- **Damage:** Ferocious Wolf, Deadly Panther or Lethal Eagle.
- **Support:** Proud Lion, whose pose after kills boosts allies' damage and speed.

Remember the chain: a slow Bear tied to an Ibex will be dragged around. Agree on a direction before each fight, as described in [chain teamwork](/co-op/chain-teamwork/).

## Do variants change the tier list?

Yes. Every beast has three variants, unlocked as that beast levels up, and some variants change its role entirely. The two confirmed Lion variants are the clearest case: the Regal Lion's crowd gives you coins, while the Proud Lion's pose buffs your allies. Among the Monkeys, the Curious Monkey trades skill choices for a bonus skill every third level, and the Clever Monkey starts with two skills and an affliction. Those suit different builds than the Vicious Monkey ranked above.

We will re-rank variants as more of them are confirmed on screen. Patches can also rebalance beasts; the developer's [post-launch roadmap](/news/post-launch-roadmap/) plans Update 1.1 for December 2026.

## What do Steam achievements say about the beasts?

Steam shows what share of players have won a run with each beast. On October 19, 2026: Ibex 16.6%, Rhino 14.9%, Wolf 10.9%, Bear 10.6%, Panther 8.9%, Eagle 4.4%, Lion 2.0% and Monkey 0.7%. This mainly tracks unlock order: Rhino and Ibex are available immediately, while the Lion and Monkey need wins on Legionnaire and Primus difficulty first. It is not evidence that the Ibex is the strongest beast.
