The October launch overview advertises online cross-play, and the official platform announcement names Steam, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S and Nintendo Switch. Steam also lists cross-platform multiplayer. These confirm a cross-play feature, but do not by themselves document every platform pairing or account workflow.

## Which platforms are announced?

Featherweight Games announced the named console versions alongside Steam, with Game Pass on Xbox and Windows PC from launch. The official Steam listing includes Windows and macOS support. Availability and subscription requirements should be checked in the store for your platform and region.

The [launch guide](/news/release-date-platforms/) covers the announced timing. A store listing or subscription inclusion is separate from the details of how two friends join one another.

## Does the game support cross-play?

Yes, the developer's launch overview explicitly advertises online cross-play. We have not verified a complete compatibility matrix or exact friend-code flow across all systems. Avoid assuming that a Steam feature label establishes every possible pairing.

Before buying specifically to play with someone, check the current in-game connection options and any official platform-specific notes. Our [multiplayer guide](/co-op/multiplayer-guide/) provides a preparation checklist without inventing menu steps.

## Is cross-save also confirmed?

The sources checked here do not establish cross-save between consoles and PC. Steam Cloud is listed, but that is not evidence of shared progression across every platform. Treat cross-play, cloud saves and cross-save as separate features.

Likewise, [Remote Play Together](/co-op/local-co-op/) is a Steam session-sharing feature and should not be used as a substitute for a platform compatibility statement.

## What remains to be verified?

Exact mixed local-and-online limits, platform-pairing restrictions and account requirements need current interface or developer evidence. If reporting a correction, include both platforms, game versions and the relevant official note or screenshot. That evidence is more useful than a general claim that multiplayer did or did not work.
