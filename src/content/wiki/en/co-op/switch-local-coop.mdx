## Is Chained Beasts on Nintendo Switch?

Yes. Chained Beasts is on Nintendo Switch: the developer launched it there on October 15, 2026, and Nintendo lists it as a download for one to four players on a single system or two to four online. Multiplayer needs a compatible controller per player, and online play needs Nintendo Switch Online.

## What does Nintendo confirm for the Switch version?

The official Nintendo UK product page describes Chained Beasts as a one-to-four-player cooperative game and provides a controller requirement for multiplayer. Use that edition's own listing rather than copying a PC input arrangement onto Switch.

| Topic | Nintendo listing information |
| --- | --- |
| Multiplayer controllers | A compatible controller is required per player |
| Online access | Paid Nintendo Switch Online membership required |
| Release date shown | October 15, 2026 on the UK page |
| Switch 2 | Compatibility listed with no issues found |
| Language list | Ten named languages, without Steam-style audio/subtitle columns |

The page does not specify in the inspected text whether one individual Joy-Con counts as a compatible controller for this game. We therefore do not promise a particular split-controller arrangement or invent a button for joining another player.

## How should a local group prepare controllers?

Allocate one compatible device to each player and confirm the console recognizes them before starting a run. Then follow the join prompts actually presented by the installed game. Verify that each person controls the intended character rather than assuming a device that navigates a system menu is already assigned correctly in the arena.

This is a preparation checklist based on the stated per-player requirement, not a claim that we tested each controller type. ## Is Nintendo Switch Online needed for couch co-op?

The product's requirement explicitly concerns online play. Do not rewrite it as a claim that the game is included in the Nintendo subscription's software catalogue. Local and online play are separate session types, so identify which mode you intend before evaluating the listed conditions.

The sources do not establish every mixed local-and-online arrangement. A local party working on one Switch is not proof that all of those players can join any remote platform combination. Our [cross-play guide](/co-op/crossplay-and-platforms/) distinguishes the advertised feature from an unverified full compatibility matrix.

## Does Switch 2 compatibility mean a separate enhanced edition?

The inspected Nintendo page lists Switch 2 compatibility with no issues found. That is a compatibility statement for the listed Switch software, not evidence of a separately verified native Switch 2 product, a particular resolution or a frame-rate upgrade.

Check the exact product and the current compatibility notes for your system. We have not benchmarked handheld or docked performance on either console.

## Are language and release details identical to Steam?

Nintendo's list names Japanese, English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Korean and Chinese. It does not break those entries into the same interface/audio/subtitle columns as Steam, so see the [language comparison](/guides/supported-languages/) before assuming the lists are interchangeable.

For timing, the developer's announced 17:00 UTC launch corresponds to October 16 at 01:00 in Singapore. Use the product's current regional access state alongside the [release-time explanation](/news/release-date-platforms/), rather than treating different displayed dates as an automatic delay.
