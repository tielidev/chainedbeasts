## What does the Xbox product page list for multiplayer?

The official Microsoft product listing identifies local and online co-op for two to four players, Xbox cross-platform co-op and Xbox Play Anywhere. The game's one-player support is documented separately by the developer and Steam description; a co-op category beginning at two does not mean solo play is unavailable.

| Listing or announcement | Supported statement |
| --- | --- |
| Xbox local co-op | Two to four players listed |
| Xbox online co-op | Two to four players listed |
| Xbox cross-platform co-op | A feature label on the Microsoft product |
| Xbox Play Anywhere | A feature label on the Microsoft product |
| Developer platform announcement | Xbox Series X|S and Windows PC Game Pass named |

The host counts toward the party maximum. These entries do not establish how many people can share one keyboard, an exact local-plus-online arrangement or a complete invitation workflow across every ecosystem.

## How should a local group prepare?

Confirm that the intended game product and input devices are available, then use the local-player prompts in the installed version to assign each person. Check each character's input before starting a serious attempt. This is general preparation; we have not tested a universal Xbox join button or a guest-account configuration for every setup.

The [local co-op guide](/co-op/local-co-op/) explains the shared-system distinction. Once the party is connected, use the [chain coordination guide](/co-op/chain-teamwork/) to agree on movement calls and a way to regroup after an interaction goes wrong.

## What does Xbox Play Anywhere prove here?

It is an official feature label for this Microsoft product. It should not be expanded into a claim that purchasing or accessing the game through Microsoft grants a Steam, PlayStation or Nintendo entitlement. The label also does not answer every cross-platform progress-transfer question on its own.

For that reason, our [cross-play overview](/co-op/crossplay-and-platforms/) keeps online compatibility, cloud synchronization and shared ownership separate. Confirm the specific editions your friends intend to use rather than treating a general cross-platform label as an exhaustive pairing table.

## How do Game Pass and cloud access differ from local co-op?

Xbox Wire announces cloud, Xbox Series X|S and PC availability and names Game Pass Ultimate and PC Game Pass. Those are access methods and subscription statements. A locally installed multiplayer session and a cloud session may have different client and device requirements, so do not assume that a working local controller setup proves all cloud arrangements.

Use the [Game Pass guide](/guides/game-pass-guide/) for the verified announcement and its limitations. This page does not benchmark cloud latency, promise access through an unlisted subscription tier or claim to have tested every streaming device.
