## Who are the bosses in Chained Beasts?

A full Chained Beasts run has **ten days**: three in each of the three acts, then a final day at the Senate. The third day of each act ends in a boss fight. We confirmed the order and the boss names from the health bars in three complete launch runs (English, Portuguese and Spanish game languages), and cross-checked them against the official Steam achievements.

| Act | Day | Boss (health-bar name) | Key achievement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I · Carthage | III | Barca, the Beast Master | Stampede (22.4%) |
| II · Germania | VI | Goliath | Deep Impact (25.2%) |
| III · Rome | IX | Praefectus | Clearing the Skies (16.8%), Don't Look Up (9.3%) |
| IV · The Senate | X | Praefectus and Machinatrix, then The Emperor | Rebel Leader: Reach The Senate (60.6%) |

In MarvGoneRogue's English run, the run tracker on Day IX shows nine battle icons with a boss icon at the end, followed by an "Act IV, The Senate" title card. That matches the developer's launch description of nine battles across three acts before a final encounter.

Percentages are Steam global unlock rates on October 19, 2026. The [achievements guide](/guides/achievements/) lists all 60.

## Barca, the Beast Master (Carthage)

**Official:** Steam's achievement Stampede reads "Cause Beastmaster Barca to be trampled to death by the Rhino".

**Video-observed:** Barca fights mounted on a rhino in the Carthage arena, and other gladiators fight alongside him. In the Portuguese-language run his bar reads "Barca, o Mestre das Feras" (Barca, the Beast Master). MarvGoneRogue baited the charging rhino by throwing objects at it while standing so Barca was in its path.

**Our tip:** the rhino's charge is the main threat and the main opportunity. Keep the arena's posts and walls between you and the charge until you can line Barca up. Surviving Carthage unlocks the Wolf, as our [all beasts guide](/beasts/all-beasts/) explains.

## Goliath (Germania)

**Official:** the developer's July open-beta announcement introduced Goliath in Germania with jumping and ground-slam attacks. The achievement Deep Impact reads "Cause an opponent to die to the Goliath's leap attack".

**Video-observed:** the Germania boss arena is snowy and crowded with tree stumps and wooden barricades. Before his big attacks, red areas appear on the ground. Late in the Portuguese-language run, several stumps were burning, which narrowed the safe space.

**Our tip:** treat a red marking as "leave now". Because the leap hurts Goliath's own side, standing so his allies sit between you and the landing spot is how Deep Impact happens. The [arena hazards guide](/combat/arena-hazards/) covers Germania's spike traps.

## The Praefectus (Rome)

**Official:** Featherweight Games calls the Praefectus its "most formidable boss yet" and says he is backed by his Praetorian Guard. Two achievements describe his fight: Clearing the Skies ("Kill the catapult before the Praefectus falls back") and Don't Look Up ("Cause an opponent to die to catapult fire during the siege").

**Video-observed:** in English the health bar reads "PRAEFECTUS". In the Portuguese-language run the arena was shelled with fire and explosions during the fight, and the Praefectus called a retreat partway through. Later he said he had to protect the Emperor and not let the gladiators reach the Senate (our translation of the Portuguese line). MarvGoneRogue's punch-heavy Bear build defeated him on Day IX in a few minutes.

**Our tip:** the catapult is a second target. Taking it out early removes constant damage and earns Clearing the Skies; leaving it up and baiting its shots onto enemies is the route to Don't Look Up. Our [acts and progression guide](/guides/acts-and-progression/) covers what is officially known about Rome.

## The Senate: Machinatrix and the Emperor

The final stage gets its own page: see our [Senate guide](/combat/the-senate/). In short, the Praefectus returns with a second boss, the **Machinatrix**, and the two share the screen with separate health bars. After that fight, **The Emperor** appears as the final boss. Surviving the Senate unlocks the Eagle.

## What about the Flail Master?

**Official:** the achievement Turning the Tide reads "Send the Flail Master flying with his own flail" (12.6% of players). A separate achievement, Whirling Dervish, rewards spinning the flail for 10 seconds.

We have not found the Flail Master's location in the footage we reviewed, so we are not placing him in a specific act. Tribune difficulty's description says "Minibosses have more health", which shows the game has minibosses, but no source we checked says whether the Flail Master is one of them.

## How do difficulty levels change boss fights?

Two difficulty descriptions mention bosses directly. **Tribune** (V) gives minibosses more health, and **Primus**, unlocked by completing Centurion, says "Bosses have more support". If a boss suddenly brings more allies, check the difficulty you selected. Our [difficulty levels guide](/guides/difficulty-levels/) lists every description we have verified.
