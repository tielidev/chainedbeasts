Weapons are only one part of Chained Beasts combat. The official description also highlights thrown objects, thrown weapons, chains and arena hazards. Our suggested approach is to choose attacks that leave you a safe next action, rather than committing to damage without a way to recover your position.

## What can you use against enemies?

The store describes throwing weapons, hurling objects and forcing enemies into hazards such as spikes. These interactions make the arena part of your toolkit. A thrown weapon can solve a distance problem but create an equipment problem, so identify what you can use afterward.

Read the [weapons and equipment guide](/combat/weapons-and-equipment/) for a simple selection process. We do not publish damage rankings without comparable measurements from a known build.

## How should you position around hazards?

Our advice is to inspect both sides of a proposed attack: where the enemy might go and where your movement might take you. In a party, the second question includes the rest of your chain. Splitting around opposite sides of an obstacle can turn a clear path into a tangle.

Agree on a movement direction and leave room to disengage. See [chain coordination](/co-op/chain-teamwork/) for a group-specific approach. This is a tactical recommendation based on the described mechanics, not a claim that every hazard affects every enemy identically.

## Should you execute every downed enemy?

The May Demo update introduced executions, and the June preview connects them with crowd approval. That makes an execution worth considering, but it does not prove that every opportunity is safe. Check the space around the target and what the rest of your group is handling.

The [crowd guide](/combat/crowd-and-executions/) separates confirmed crowd interactions from unsupported bonus formulas. Avoid relying on precise animation or immunity timings unless your current skill description establishes them.

## What should you review after losing?

Ask whether the cause was positioning, an unfamiliar action, an equipment gap or a drawback you had accepted earlier. Different causes need different adjustments. The [afflictions guide](/combat/afflictions-and-healing/) helps identify when a recovery decision, rather than the final hit, changed the run.
