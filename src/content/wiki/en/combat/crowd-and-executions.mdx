## What are executions?

The May Demo update introduced executions as a way to finish downed enemies. That establishes the feature, but it does not provide a verified control binding for every platform or a complete timing chart. Follow the current tutorial and prompts for the actual input.

Read the [controls guide](/guides/controls-and-controller/) if you are changing input devices. A recorded Demo clip is useful context but may use an older interface or build.

## When should you attempt one?

Our tactical recommendation is to consider the situation around the target first. Other enemies, hazards and your team's movement can make a finishing opportunity less attractive. An available interaction does not automatically make it the safest next move.

In co-op, say what you intend to do so your partners can adjust rather than pull you toward a different fight. The [chain teamwork guide](/co-op/chain-teamwork/) gives a simple framework for those calls.

## Does an execution always make you invulnerable?

Do not assume that. The July beta post described a named skill, Untouchable, with a temporary invulnerability effect after an execution. A benefit attached to a particular skill is not proof of a baseline rule for every character in every version.

Read the skill you actually have and the current effect text. This guide does not convert an older beta duration into a universal launch guarantee.

## How should crowd support affect a run?

Treat it as a system to learn alongside equipment and recovery. Observe which actions the game visibly rewards, then build a plan you can repeat without sacrificing positioning. Review [combat basics](/combat/combat-basics/) and [skills](/combat/skills-and-shopping/) before chasing a bonus at any cost.

## How can you decide whether to finish a downed enemy?

Make the decision in the context of the whole arena. A downed target is only one part of the situation; nearby opponents, environmental hazards and the chain linking your party still matter. Our suggested checklist is deliberately independent of an unverified timing window.

1. Identify the other threats that can reach the area.
2. Check where allies intend to move before committing.
3. Read any relevant execution skill you actually have.
4. Decide where to go after the interaction rather than stopping the plan at the finishing move.

If the party cannot agree on space around the target, regroup before making the encounter depend on the maneuver. That is tactical advice, not a statement that a particular animation can always be cancelled or that every target permits the same interaction.
