## Why do early videos show a different shield system?

The developer's “What's Next” update explicitly contrasts the public playtest with planned Demo changes. In the earlier design, everyone began with a shield; blocking consumed some stamina and holding the shield slowed stamina recovery. The described rework removed starting shields and made them pickups with durability.

That history is useful when an old video does not match your current starting equipment. It does not establish that every detail is unchanged in the launch build. This page records the source's comparison rather than claiming we tested a current defensive rotation.

| System element | Earlier public-playtest description | Described rework |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starting equipment | Players began with shields | Shields became arena pickups |
| Block resource | Stamina affected blocking | Blocking described without stamina cost |
| Repeated hits | Earlier stamina rules applied | Hits reduce shield durability; it can break |
| Enemy response | Not given as the new design's rule | A blocked attacker is briefly stunned |
| Attack recovery | Earlier advice could wait for an animation | The update describes cancelling attacks with a block |

The source does not provide a complete launch durability table, a current stun duration or a boss-by-boss blocking rule. Those values should not be reconstructed from the general rework description.

## How were replacement shields described?

The update says players could pick up a replacement after killing a shield-carrying enemy. That is the documented acquisition example. It does not establish a guaranteed shield at every location or a universal drop rule for every enemy that appears to carry one.

When evaluating your current equipment, inspect the item and tutorial information actually available. A defensive plan that assumes unlimited use can fail if the design relies on durability. Our [equipment guide](/combat/weapons-and-equipment/) explains the broader question of what remains available after an interaction.

## What does the attack-cancellation note mean for a guide?

It establishes that the developer described blocking as a way to cancel an attack in that rework. It does not supply an input binding for every platform, frame data or proof that every animation and enemy interaction can be interrupted in the same way.

Practice the action shown by your installed tutorial before making a difficult encounter depend on it. If current behavior differs, record the version and exact action. The [controls overview](/guides/controls-and-controller/) is the starting point for checking bindings, while [combat fundamentals](/combat/combat-basics/) covers the space you need after an action.

## How can you avoid using an outdated shield recommendation?

Check whether its evidence comes from the public playtest, initial Demo, updated Demo, beta or full game. Keep the source date beside any precise resource or timing claim. A recommendation based on always starting with a shield should not silently become a universal launch starting-loadout rule.

This historical reference remains deliberately narrower than a complete blocking walkthrough. A current guide needs the actual item text, the relevant build and repeatable observations before adding durability values, precise timing or boss-specific guarantees.
