## What is the Senate in Chained Beasts?

The Senate is the **final stage** of every Chained Beasts run. After nine days across Carthage, Germania and Rome, the game shows a title card reading "Act IV, The Senate", and the run moves to **Day X**. The end-of-run screen in MarvGoneRogue's launch video confirms the label: "Act IV, Day X".

The Steam achievement **Rebel Leader** ("Reach The Senate") was unlocked by 60.6% of players four days after launch, so most players who buy the game have already seen it. Winning it is harder: the beast-specific "Free as a ..." achievements, which need a full win, sit between 0.7% and 16.8%.

The developer's own launch description, nine battles across three acts with bosses before a final encounter, matches this structure. Our [acts and progression guide](/guides/acts-and-progression/) covers the three acts before it.

## Who do you fight at the Senate?

We reviewed the Senate in three launch runs, in English, Portuguese and Spanish game languages. All three show the same order:

| Phase | On-screen boss name (English) | What we observed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Praefectus + Machinatrix | Two health bars side by side; the Praefectus returns from Rome with a second boss |
| 2 | The Emperor | A single health bar after the first fight; defeating him triggers "Victory!" |

**Video-observed details:**

- Day X begins outside: a plaza with a burning statue, then a corridor to a columned building, before the arena.
- As the first fight starts, a speech bubble reads "Seize them!".
- The Senate arena is a pale marble ring surrounded by spectators, unlike the sand of Carthage or the snow of Germania.
- In Spanish the bosses are "Prefecto", "Maquinatriz" and "El Emperador"; in Portuguese, "Prefeito", "Maquinatriz" and "O Imperador".

We are not publishing an attack-by-attack breakdown. The footage shows crowded fights at a small scale, and we would rather say less than describe moves we cannot confirm.

## Why does the Praefectus come back?

In Rome, the Praefectus is the Day IX boss. In the Portuguese-language run he called a retreat during that fight and said he had to protect the Emperor and stop the gladiators from reaching the Senate (our translation). The Senate is where that story pays off: he returns beside the Machinatrix, and the Emperor follows. Our [boss guide](/combat/bosses/) covers his Rome fight and its catapult achievements.

## How should you prepare on Night IX?

**Video-observed:** in Pe Pra's run, Night IX offered only the Healer, while earlier nights also included the Trainer and, on some nights, the Merchant or Surgeon. In MarvGoneRogue's Night IX, the Healer's shelf showed three potions, each listing a heart value and an affliction (Brain Fog III, Paranoia II and Unsteady II in that run), plus a "Water" card showing 0 and the condition "Must have 20 or more health".

**Our tips:**

- Arrive with your build finished. If Night IX only offers the Healer, Night VIII is your last chance to train and shop, so spend there with the Senate in mind.
- Read each potion's affliction before drinking on Night IX. An affliction such as Brain Fog ("Delay in movement input") is a heavy price before two boss fights in a row.
- Expect two boss fights in a row. A build that kills one target fast, such as MarvGoneRogue's Bear with Brass Knuckles, still has to handle two bosses at once in phase 1.

Our [afflictions and healing guide](/combat/afflictions-and-healing/) explains how remedies and drawbacks interact.

## What do you get for beating the Senate?

- **The Eagle.** Surviving the Senate unlocks the Eagle beast; see [all beasts](/beasts/all-beasts/).
- **A beast achievement.** Each win counts for that beast's "Free as a ..." achievement.
- **Harder difficulties.** After MarvGoneRogue's Centurion win, the game showed "Unlocked: Primus" with the description "Bosses have more support; Can't choose first skill". See [difficulty levels](/guides/difficulty-levels/).
- **Cosmetics.** The same screen also unlocked "Evocatus", described as a new cosmetic available for all beasts.

For the full list of run and boss achievements, see the [achievements guide](/guides/achievements/).
