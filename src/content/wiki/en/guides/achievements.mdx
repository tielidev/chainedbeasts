## How many achievements are in Chained Beasts?

Chained Beasts has **60 Steam achievements**, and none of them are hidden. We copied every name and description below from the official Steam achievement page, and took each global unlock rate from Steam's public achievement-percentage API on October 19, 2026, four days after launch. Expect the percentages to rise as more players finish runs.

How to read this guide:

- **Official** text is the Steam description, quoted as written.
- **Video-observed** hints come from launch gameplay we reviewed frame by frame (sources at the end).
- **Our tip** marks editorial advice. We have not played every achievement ourselves, so treat tips as a starting plan, not a guaranteed method.

Console trophy and Xbox achievement lists are not covered here, because we have not verified them.

## Campaign and run achievements

These track how far you get. The campaign runs through Carthage, Germania and Rome, then a final stage called the Senate. Our [acts and progression guide](/guides/acts-and-progression/) covers the route, and the [Senate guide](/combat/the-senate/) covers the last fight.

| Achievement | Official description | Global % |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Can't Stand The Heat | Survive Carthage | 91.3% |
| Out of the Cold | Survive Germania | 74.5% |
| Rebel Leader | Reach The Senate | 60.6% |
| A Quick Escape | Win a run while spending fewer than 15 minutes in battles | 2.0% |
| Untouchable | Complete Carthage without taking any damage | 0.9% |
| King of the Gods | Win a run on Jupiter difficulty | 0.1% |

**Video-observed:** the end-of-run screen shows a "Total Battle Time" counter, which is the clock that matters for A Quick Escape. In MarvGoneRogue's launch run the Centurion win took 32:08 of battle time, so the 15-minute target needs a much faster build.

**Our tip:** go for Untouchable on a low difficulty with a defensive beast. The Armored Rhino takes 30% less damage, but the achievement asks for none at all, so blocking and dodging matter more than armor. King of the Gods sits at the top of the difficulty ladder; see [difficulty levels](/guides/difficulty-levels/) for the names we have verified.

## Boss achievements

Four of these name a specific boss or set piece. They are also the best evidence of how each fight works; our [boss guide](/combat/bosses/) covers the encounters in order.

| Achievement | Official description | Global % |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep Impact | Cause an opponent to die to the Goliath's leap attack | 25.2% |
| Stampede | Cause Beastmaster Barca to be trampled to death by the Rhino | 22.4% |
| Clearing the Skies | Kill the catapult before the Praefectus falls back | 16.8% |
| Turning the Tide | Send the Flail Master flying with his own flail | 12.6% |
| Don't Look Up | Cause an opponent to die to catapult fire during the siege | 9.3% |
| Who's The Boss | Kill a Boss within 10 seconds of first damaging them | 2.3% |

**Video-observed:** in Carthage, Barca fights mounted on a rhino. MarvGoneRogue baited the charging Rhino by throwing objects at it and standing so Barca was in its path. In Pe Pra's Rome run, the Praefectus arena was shelled with fire and explosions while he fought, and partway through he called a retreat (the Portuguese line was "Recuar!"). Clearing the Skies asks you to destroy the catapult before he falls back, so the catapult is worth attacking early.

**Our tip:** for Deep Impact and Don't Look Up, stand so enemies are between you and the boss's area attack. Who's The Boss rewards burst damage; MarvGoneRogue's Bear build with Brass Knuckles took bosses down in a few hits.

## Camp, shop and affliction achievements

Between fights you visit the Healer, Trainer, Merchant and Surgeon. These achievements reward pushing those systems to extremes.

| Achievement | Official description | Global % |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No Rest Day | Train to exhaustion | 18.2% |
| Medical Marvel | Win a run while suffering afflictions totalling at least 20 skulls | 7.6% |
| Dead Weight | Amputate a body part | 7.2% |
| Majority Stakeholder | Individually earn at least 400 coins in a run | 7.2% |
| Moneybags | Buy out the Merchant's entire store at once | 3.1% |
| Overtrained | Win a run after reaching level 20 at the Trainer | 2.2% |
| Ultra Hydrated | Win a run while only ever drinking water | 1.2% |
| Untrained | Win a run without levelling up at the Trainer | 0.1% |
| Collector | Purchase every type of item from the Merchant | 0.1% |
| Test Subject | Suffer the maximum level of every affliction | 0.0% |
| Talented | Learn every skill | 0.0% |
| Scholarly | Discover every Codex entry | 0.0% |

**Video-observed:** the Healer's shelf includes a "Water" option next to its potions, with the condition "Must have 20 or more health" and a heart value of 0, while each potion lists a heart value and an affliction. Ultra Hydrated asks you to drink only water, so it means passing on every potion for a whole run. In open-beta footage, Legate difficulty's description included "Drinking water is not clean", so check your difficulty's text before planning a water-only run. The Merchant's stock in MarvGoneRogue's run was eight cards, which is what Moneybags asks you to buy at once.

**Our tip:** Untrained and Overtrained are opposite extremes, so plan them as separate runs. Training carries risk; Legionnaire's description warns that "Training too hard causes affliction". Our [afflictions and healing guide](/combat/afflictions-and-healing/) and [skills, training and shopping guide](/combat/skills-and-shopping/) explain the trade-offs.

## Which achievements should you go for first?

Our suggested order, based on the unlock rates above:

1. **Play normally first.** X, L, C, Destruction of Property, Two For One, Pincushion and Can't Stand The Heat come naturally; each is held by more than 75% of players.
2. **Then target the boss tricks.** Stampede, Deep Impact and Clearing the Skies need you to set up a boss fight deliberately, but each is a single attempt per run.
3. **Then the beast wins.** Each new beast you unlock adds a "Free as a ..." target.
4. **Leave the 0.0% group for last.** Weapon Master, Talented, Scholarly and Test Subject need many runs.

For general advice that helps with most of these, see our [tips and tricks](/guides/tips/).
