Chained Beasts is about surviving a sequence of arena fights while managing the problems that follow you out of each one. Start with the tutorial, learn how your chain affects movement, and resist treating every encounter as a race for damage. This guide separates official mechanics from our suggested approach.

## What should you learn before your first run?

Use the tutorial to practice movement, attacking and the interactions shown by your current build. Check the in-game bindings rather than relying on an old Demo key chart: controller layouts and platform prompts can differ. Steam lists full controller support and recommends a gamepad.

Spend your first fight noticing where your character actually ends up after an attack. With friends, also watch the chain. A route that is clear for one fighter can pull another around a pillar or into a hazard. Read the [controls guide](/guides/controls-and-controller/) before changing devices.

## How should you approach the arena?

The developer confirms that weapons can be thrown and that arena objects and hazards can hurt enemies. Our recommendation is to keep an exit route and a replacement weapon in mind before committing. A dramatic throw is less useful if it leaves you empty-handed beside several enemies.

In a group, agree on one direction of movement before scattering. Use the [chain teamwork guide](/co-op/chain-teamwork/) to turn that habit into coordinated positioning. In solo play, begin with the [solo guide](/guides/solo-guide/) instead of copying a four-player formation.

## What matters between battles?

Recovery involves trade-offs: healing can add afflictions, while shopping and skills affect your next fight. Before choosing, read the actual effect and consider who in your group will bear the consequence. An injury that changes vision or movement can alter a plan even when your health improves.

Our suggested first-run goal is simple: learn one arena interaction, one safe way to recover your position, and one decision you would change next time. The [healing guide](/combat/afflictions-and-healing/) explains why health alone does not tell the whole story.

## Which version does this guide cover?

This is an introduction based on official launch information checked on October 15, 2026. It is not a claim of a completed hands-on walkthrough. The full game is announced with three acts and ten difficulty levels; old Demo guides describe a smaller experience.
