## How many difficulty levels does Chained Beasts have?

Chained Beasts has **ten difficulty levels**. Featherweight Games' launch overview announces "10 increasingly difficult levels", and the select screen shows the current level as a Roman numeral above its name, with arrows to step between unlocked levels.

We read the names and modifier lines below directly from the select screen in gameplay videos, and label each one by the build it came from. Levels we have not seen yet are marked as unverified rather than guessed.

Two caveats. First, Legate comes from open-beta footage, so its number or wording may have changed for launch. Second, we saw Primus only on its unlock card, which shows no numeral, so we have not placed it in a numbered row. Jupiter's position is likewise unconfirmed.

## How do you unlock higher difficulties?

You unlock difficulty levels by **winning runs**. At the end of MarvGoneRogue's launch run on Centurion, the game marked the challenge "Complete Centurion" and then showed "Unlocked: Primus" with its two modifiers.

Some difficulties also unlock beasts. According to the beast-select screen, a win on **Legionnaire** unlocks the **Lion** and a win on **Primus** unlocks the **Monkey**. Our [all beasts guide](/beasts/all-beasts/) lists every condition.

## What do the modifiers mean in practice?

The modifiers are short, but they point at specific systems:

- **Half starting health (Centurion).** You begin each run already hurt, so the Healer's trade-offs matter from the first night. See [afflictions and healing](/combat/afflictions-and-healing/).
- **A harder crowd (Centurion, Tribune).** Crowd approval is harder to earn, and on Tribune the crowd throws pots. The [crowd and executions guide](/combat/crowd-and-executions/) explains why the crowd matters.
- **Training risk (Legionnaire).** Overtraining causes an affliction, so the Trainer is no longer free value. See [skills, training and shopping](/combat/skills-and-shopping/).
- **Rent in Rome (Tribune).** The owner demands rent once you reach Rome, the third act. We have not seen the amount, so keep some coins in reserve.
- **Tougher fights (Tribune, Legate, Primus).** Minibosses get more health, normal enemies hit harder and bosses get more support. Our [boss guide](/combat/bosses/) covers each boss.
- **Dirty water (Legate, open beta).** The Healer offers a Water option next to its potions; on Legate the description warns the water is not clean. We have not seen what that does in practice.

## What about the old Demo difficulty names?

The June updated-Demo announcement named four options: Pleb, Legionnaire, Centurion and Hades. Legionnaire and Centurion survive at launch as II and III, but Pleb and Hades do not appear in the launch footage we reviewed, which starts at I Tutorial. Treat Demo-era videos and guides that mention Pleb or Hades as historical. An earlier development post also described simpler training on Pleb than on harder settings; that is design history, not a launch rule.

## How should you pick a difficulty?

Our recommendation:

1. **Finish a run on Tutorial first.** It is the level a new save starts on, and a full run teaches the camp loop of Healer, Trainer, Merchant and Surgeon.
2. **Move to Legionnaire** once you can survive Carthage reliably. The Lion unlock is a good reason to clear it.
3. **Treat Centurion as the real test.** Starting at half health changes every healing decision.
4. **Change one thing at a time.** In co-op, raising the difficulty while every player also changes beast makes it hard to see what went wrong. The [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/) has practice goals for a new group.

The top achievement, King of the Gods, needs a Jupiter win. See the [achievements guide](/guides/achievements/) for every run challenge.
