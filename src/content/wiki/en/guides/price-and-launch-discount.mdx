## What price did the developer announce?

The October launch-price post names a regular US$19.99 price and a US$12.99 launch offer, described as a 35% discount for two weeks. The figures belong to that announcement. We have not observed a completed purchase or verified a final checkout in every region and storefront.

| Price information | Developer announcement |
| --- | --- |
| Regular US-dollar price | US$19.99 |
| Launch offer | US$12.99 |
| Discount described | 35% |
| Duration described | Two weeks at launch |

Do not convert these into an assumed local-currency price using an exchange rate and label it official. A regional store can use its own pricing and display rules. The amount and offer state shown by your intended product are the relevant purchase check.

## When does the launch offer start or finish?

The source describes a launch promotion lasting two weeks, but this guide does not invent an exact regional end timestamp. The developer separately announced October 15 at 17:00 UTC for launch. That is October 16 at 01:00 in Singapore, which explains why some readers see a different calendar date.

Read the [release-time explanation](/news/release-date-platforms/) and inspect the current store offer. A discount announced before release does not mean every region has already unlocked or that the offer will remain after its stated window.

## Is the Demo a permanently free version?

The developer said the Carthage Demo would be taken offline when the full game launched. Its temporary availability should not be presented as a promise of an ongoing free edition containing the full campaign.

The [Demo comparison](/guides/demo-vs-full-game/) describes the separate products and their content. Use official store links rather than a third-party download page that claims to provide a current free full version.

## How is Game Pass access different from a purchase price?

The official Game Pass announcement names supported Microsoft access methods and subscription tiers. That is a separate question from buying a particular Steam or console product. A subscription statement should not be rewritten as a purchase entitlement in every storefront.

See [Game Pass access](/guides/game-pass-guide/) for the verified announcement. Before arranging a party, also check [cross-play and platform distinctions](/co-op/crossplay-and-platforms/): the availability of two editions does not by itself document how they connect or share progress.

## What should you confirm at the store?

Check the product name, platform, account region, displayed currency, current offer and any taxes or conditions shown before purchase. If a price differs from this page, compare it with the current official product rather than assume either figure applies worldwide. This page records an announcement and does not monitor prices automatically.
