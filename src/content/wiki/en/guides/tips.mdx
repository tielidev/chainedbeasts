## What are the best Chained Beasts tips for beginners?

These tips come from three kinds of evidence, labeled as we go: the official Steam achievement list (each achievement proves a mechanic exists), the developer's announcements, and launch gameplay we reviewed on video. Where a tip is our own judgment, we say so.

## Combat and the chain

**1. The chain is a weapon, not just a handicap.** Steam achievements reward squeezing an opponent with your chain ("Pop!") and tripping 100 opponents with it ("Trip Master"). The developer describes synchronized teams tripping enemies while uncoordinated teams wrap around pillars. Agree on a direction before a fight; see [chain teamwork](/co-op/chain-teamwork/).

**2. Trip whole formations.** The achievements "Formation Breaker" and "Down the Line" ask you to trip, or hit with one attack, every opponent in a formation. Shielded lines are not walls; the chain can sweep them.

**3. Throw enemies into the arena.** Achievements exist for impaling an opponent on a spike wall, throwing one onto a spike plate and executing one with an explosive barrel. Fighting next to a hazard is an opportunity if you control who ends up in it. Our [arena hazards guide](/combat/arena-hazards/) covers what to watch.

**4. Nothing is useless.** You can kill an opponent with an empty bow, kill one with its own arm, and earn an achievement for having 10 projectiles stuck in you at once. Dismembered limbs and spent weapons are still things to hit or throw. The Ferocious Wolf even deals +200% damage with enemy body parts.

**5. Wheels and big swings hit crowds.** "Drive By" rewards hitting three enemies with one wheel, and "Two For One" rewards killing two opponents with one attack. When enemies bunch up, look for an attack that reaches all of them.

## Bosses and set pieces

**6. Make the Rhino trample its master.** In Carthage, the "Stampede" achievement asks you to get Beastmaster Barca trampled to death by the Rhino. Video creators bait the charging Rhino by throwing objects at it, then stand so the boss is in its path.

**7. Turn boss attacks against their allies.** "Deep Impact" rewards an opponent dying to the Goliath's leap attack, and "Don't Look Up" rewards enemies dying to catapult fire during the Praefectus siege. Boss area attacks hurt the boss's own side; stand so enemies are between you and the impact.

**8. Kill the catapult early.** "Clearing the Skies" asks you to destroy the catapult before the Praefectus falls back. Removing the siege engine early takes away a constant source of damage. Our [acts and progression guide](/guides/acts-and-progression/) covers Rome and the Praefectus.

**9. Watch the crowd.** "Pay Attention" exists for dying to an object thrown by the crowd, and higher difficulties add an angry crowd that throws pots. Crowd approval can help you, as the [crowd guide](/combat/crowd-and-executions/) explains, but thrown objects can also finish you off.

## Healing, training and shopping

**10. Every heal is a trade.** Recovering between rounds can add afflictions that affect movement, perception or decisions. Read the drawback before accepting a heal. "Ultra Hydrated" shows a run can be won while only ever drinking water, so full health is not mandatory. See [afflictions and healing](/combat/afflictions-and-healing/).

**11. Do not overtrain.** "No Rest Day" rewards training to exhaustion, and on Legionnaire difficulty the select screen warns that training too hard causes an affliction. The Trainer can take you to at least level 20 ("Overtrained"), but each extra session carries risk.

**12. Plan your coins.** The Merchant sells items between fights, and "Moneybags" exists for buying out its entire stock at once. Spend on what fixes your current problem; see [skills, training and shopping](/combat/skills-and-shopping/).

**13. Amputation is an option.** "Dead Weight" asks you to amputate a body part at the Surgeon. Losing a limb sounds bad, but it can remove a worse problem. Read what the surgery changes before you agree.

## Beasts and progression

**14. Stick with one beast.** Each beast has three variants. Variants II and III unlock when that beast reaches level II and III, and XP is earned per beast. Rotating every run slows every unlock. See [all beasts and unlocks](/beasts/all-beasts/).

**15. Start forgiving, then specialize.** Our [tier list](/beasts/tier-list/) rates the Armored Rhino (30% less damage taken, longer-lasting shields) and the Hulking Bear as the safest picks. Once you can win reliably, try glass cannons like the Deadly Panther.

## Which tips matter most in co-op?

In [multiplayer](/co-op/multiplayer-guide/), everyone shares the consequences of one person's movement. Call your direction before each fight, decide who carries the durable beast, and do not all accept heals with the same affliction risk in the same round. If you play on different platforms, check the [crossplay](/co-op/crossplay-and-platforms/) option in the lobby first.
