## When is the Chained Beasts release date?

Chained Beasts released on October 15, 2026, at 10:00 AM PDT (17:00 UTC) on Steam, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S and Nintendo Switch, with Game Pass for Xbox and Windows PC from day one. Featherweight Games confirmed the Steam launch in its "Chained Beasts is available NOW" announcement that day.

The September platform announcement states October 15 at 10:00 AM PDT, equivalent to 17:00 UTC. Regional store displays can use different dates or time zones. Check the store's current purchase or play state when deciding whether your particular version is available.

The date alone can be misleading for readers east of UTC. Our displayed article date is October 15, when these official details were checked, rather than a claim that a launch-time hands-on review is complete.

## Where is the game launching?

The developer names Steam, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S and Nintendo Switch. It also announced Game Pass inclusion for Xbox and Windows PC from day one. The Steam listing identifies Windows and macOS support.

For playing with friends on different systems, see the [cross-play overview](/co-op/crossplay-and-platforms/). Platform availability alone is not a full matchmaking compatibility chart.

## What happens to the Demo?

The developer said the Carthage Demo would be taken offline when the full game launches. Do not rely on an old article promising a permanently available free Demo. Store availability at the moment you visit remains the final check.

The full campaign adds the named chapters Germania and Rome beyond Carthage. Our [progression guide](/guides/acts-and-progression/) distinguishes those launch details from older Demo coverage.

## Why can stores show October 15 or October 16?

The announced global time crosses midnight in some regions. Use the time zone as well as the calendar date when comparing an article, a developer announcement and your local storefront.

| Reference zone | Announced equivalent |
| --- | --- |
| UTC | October 15, 17:00 |
| Pacific Daylight Time | October 15, 10:00 AM |
| Singapore, UTC+8 | October 16, 01:00 |

This table converts the developer's announcement; it does not override a platform's actual access state. If a store still shows a countdown, record the platform and region before concluding that another reader's date is wrong.

## Who made Chained Beasts?

Chained Beasts is developed and published by Featherweight Games. The studio's own site says it was founded in 2014 by Dylan Bevis and Tim Kaldor and describes itself as a small studio. Its earlier releases include Rodeo Stampede, Botworld Adventure, Botworld Odyssey, Skiing Yeti Mountain and Auto Pirates, which it says have been downloaded more than 300 million times combined.

In the launch announcement, co-director Tim Kaldor says Chained Beasts was the first game the team set out to make, 12 years earlier. It began as a 48-hour game jam project called Enterchained, inspired by the chained-up scene in the film Gladiator, and was set aside after six months before the team returned to the concept years later. Featherweight also took the game to ANZ IndieFest, a showcase of games made by teams in Australia and New Zealand.
